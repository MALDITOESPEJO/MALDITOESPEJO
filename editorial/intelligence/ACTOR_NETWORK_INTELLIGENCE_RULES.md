# ACTOR & NETWORK INTELLIGENCE RULES

## AN-01 — Actor is not attribution
The existence or identification of an actor does not establish responsibility for an event, claim or operation.

## AN-02 — Observation before inference
Record observable behavior before constructing an analytical relationship.

## AN-03 — Attribution is separate
Actor identity, relationship and responsibility must be assessed as separate evidentiary questions.

## AN-04 — Similarity is a lead
Username similarity, writing style, imagery, ideology, geography or thematic alignment cannot alone establish identity or control.

## AN-05 — Coordination requires evidence
Synchronized activity may justify investigation but is not automatically proof of coordination.

## AN-06 — Network centrality is not culpability
Highly connected nodes may simply be major publishers, aggregators or legitimate institutions.

## AN-07 — Common lineage reduces independence
Multiple actors repeating one upstream source do not create multiple independent evidence lineages.

## AN-08 — State attribution has a high threshold
Attribution to a government or state-linked entity requires evidence beyond political alignment or content similarity.

## AN-09 — Alternative explanations are mandatory
Material attribution hypotheses must document plausible non-malicious or non-coordinated explanations and how they were assessed.

## AN-10 — Public and private identity
Do not expose private personal information solely to resolve an editorial hypothesis. Necessity and public-interest relevance must be established.

## AN-11 — Anonymous means anonymous unless necessary
Do not identify anonymous or pseudonymous actors when identification is not essential to the public-interest finding or is insufficiently evidenced.

## AN-12 — Impersonation is distinct
An account claiming to represent another actor must not be attributed to that actor without evidence of control or authorization.

## AN-13 — Temporal scope matters
Relationships and affiliations can change. Every material relationship should have an applicable temporal scope where possible.

## AN-14 — Relationship provenance is mandatory
Every consequential network edge must be traceable to evidence.

## AN-15 — Confidence is separate
Identity confidence, relationship confidence and attribution confidence must not be collapsed into one score.

## AN-16 — Fact-checking does not establish origin
A fact-check can establish or challenge a claim without establishing who originated or coordinated the narrative.

## AN-17 — Automation only prioritizes
Automated graph analysis may detect anomalies and prioritize review but cannot make consequential attribution decisions.

## AN-18 — Preserve disputes
Conflicting attribution assessments must remain visible and auditable.

## AN-19 — Historical states remain preserved
Later identity or relationship changes must not erase what was previously observed or published.

## AN-20 — Human editorial gate
Any consequential allegation concerning responsibility, coordination, manipulation or state linkage requires human review before publication.
