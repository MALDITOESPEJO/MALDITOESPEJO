# SOURCE PORTFOLIO STRATEGY RULES

### SP-01 — Portfolio, not source count
Portfolio strength must not be inferred from the number of registered sources.

### SP-02 — Lineage-level independence
Independence must be assessed at the underlying evidentiary lineage level.

### SP-03 — Function-specific coverage
Coverage is assessed against an editorial function, topic, jurisdiction and evidence class.

### SP-04 — Primary evidence priority
Portfolio design must preserve access to primary evidence for consequential claims whenever such evidence exists.

### SP-05 — Detection and verification separation
Multiple detection paths do not automatically constitute multiple verification lineages.

### SP-06 — Verification diversity
Where risk justifies it, consequential claims should have access to more than one appropriate verification pathway.

### SP-07 — Operational redundancy
Technical fallback is valuable but does not constitute evidentiary independence by itself.

### SP-08 — False redundancy alert
Shared dispatches, datasets, witnesses, documents or parent feeds must be treated as common lineage where appropriate.

### SP-09 — Critical uniqueness
A unique authoritative source is not automatically a portfolio defect. Document the uniqueness explicitly.

### SP-10 — Geographic specificity
Global coverage must not be assumed from sources with weak local evidence.

### SP-11 — Institutional specificity
Coverage of a topic does not guarantee coverage of the institutions that generate the relevant facts.

### SP-12 — Temporal fitness
A source suitable for historical analysis may be unsuitable for breaking detection, and vice versa.

### SP-13 — Methodological concentration
Excessive dependence on one methodology must be treated as a strategic risk when alternative methodologies are feasible and material.

### SP-14 — Strategic dependency
Loss of a single lineage that materially affects consequential editorial capability triggers portfolio review.

### SP-15 — Acquisition follows gap evidence
New sources should be acquired primarily to close demonstrated coverage, verification, independence or resilience gaps.

### SP-16 — Duplication reduction
Duplicate sources may be reduced when they add no meaningful independence, coverage, detection or continuity value.

### SP-17 — No artificial balance
Portfolio balance does not require equal numbers of sources, institutions or viewpoints.

### SP-18 — No political ranking
Portfolio strategy must not be used to rank political viewpoints or institutions by ideological preference.

### SP-19 — Historical preservation
Portfolio changes must preserve the historical source and evidence relationships used in prior cases.

### SP-20 — Human strategic gate
Material portfolio actions require human editorial review.

## Canonical portfolio flow

`EDITORIAL REQUIREMENT → COVERAGE ASSESSMENT → DEPENDENCY ANALYSIS → LINEAGE ANALYSIS → PERFORMANCE/HEALTH REVIEW → RESILIENCE REVIEW → PORTFOLIO ACTION → GOVERNANCE → MONITORING`
