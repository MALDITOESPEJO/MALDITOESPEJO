# EDITORIAL STORY INTELLIGENCE RULES

## ST-01 — Signal is not story
A detected signal may justify investigation without constituting a publishable story.

## ST-02 — Central proposition first
Every consequential story must identify the smallest material proposition that needs to be established.

## ST-03 — Evidence before angle
An attractive angle cannot compensate for weak evidence.

## ST-04 — News value does not lower the evidence threshold
Public interest, urgency or consequence never authorizes publication of unsupported claims.

## ST-05 — Attribution must survive transformation
Claims originating with an actor or source remain attributed unless independently established.

## ST-06 — Headline cannot exceed evidence
The headline must not assert more than the body and evidence can establish.

## ST-07 — Alternative angles remain visible
Where evidence supports materially different interpretations, preserve them until human editorial review.

## ST-08 — Open questions are first-class objects
Material unknowns must be recorded rather than silently filled with inference.

## ST-09 — Verification must be actionable
Every material unresolved question should identify the evidence and method capable of resolving it.

## ST-10 — Common lineage is not corroboration
Repeated reporting from one upstream document, dispatch, dataset or witness remains one evidentiary lineage.

## ST-11 — Causality requires domain-appropriate evidence
Chronology, correlation or narrative coherence alone cannot justify causal language.

## ST-12 — Forecasts remain forecasts
Warnings, scenarios, probabilities and models must not be presented as events that have occurred.

## ST-13 — Impact must be evidenced
Potential consequences belong in analysis; observed consequences require appropriate evidence.

## ST-14 — Legal and privacy risks are explicit
The story package must identify material legal, attribution and privacy risks before publication.

## ST-15 — No sensational optimization
The system must not select an angle or headline solely because it maximizes attention or engagement.

## ST-16 — Publication readiness is not authorization
`READY_FOR_DECISION` means the package is mature enough for human decision, not that publication is approved.

## ST-17 — Preserve uncertainty
Material uncertainty must remain visible in both editorial reasoning and final language.

## ST-18 — Preserve history
Later evidence must not be back-projected into the original story assessment without an explicit temporal record.

## ST-19 — Corrections remain linked
If evidence changes after publication, the story remains linked to its prior evidence, decision and publication versions.

## ST-20 — Human responsibility
The final editorial decision belongs to a human editor who accepts responsibility for what is published.

## Core rule

> Build the story around what can be established, not around what would make the strongest story.
