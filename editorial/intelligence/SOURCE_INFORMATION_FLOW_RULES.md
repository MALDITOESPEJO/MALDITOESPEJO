# SOURCE INFORMATION FLOW RULES

## IF-01 — Origin is not publication
Record the underlying event, observation, document or dataset separately from every subsequent report.

## IF-02 — First known is not absolute first
`FIRST_DETECTION` means the earliest detection available to MALDITOESPEJO unless stronger evidence establishes an earlier origin.

## IF-03 — Propagation is not corroboration
A downstream report that repeats upstream information does not create independent evidentiary support.

## IF-04 — Preserve transformations
Record material changes in wording, certainty, attribution, timing, geography, numbers and context.

## IF-05 — Translation is a transformation
Translations preserve the upstream relationship and must not be treated as independent evidence merely because language differs.

## IF-06 — Aggregation is a transformation
Aggregators may improve discovery but do not automatically create independent corroboration.

## IF-07 — Attribution must survive
When a claim is attributed upstream, removal of that attribution downstream is a material transformation requiring review.

## IF-08 — Certainty drift is material
An allegation, estimate, forecast or preliminary result must not silently become a categorical fact during propagation.

## IF-09 — Chronology is immutable
Later information cannot be used to imply that the same information was available earlier.

## IF-10 — Corrections propagate
A material upstream correction or retraction requires impact assessment of dependent downstream claims and publications.

## IF-11 — Cascades are not coordination proof
Large propagation cascades may be legitimate. Coordination or malicious intent requires independent evidence.

## IF-12 — Bottlenecks are operational risks
Single-provider or single-origin dependencies must be visible even where the underlying source is authoritative.

## IF-13 — Velocity is not reliability
Fast detection is valuable but does not replace verification.

## IF-14 — Independent corroboration milestone
Record separately the first genuinely independent corroboration of the proposition.

## IF-15 — Publication must be traceable backwards
Consequential published assertions require a recoverable chain from publication to decision, verification, evidence and origin.

## IF-16 — Broken trace triggers review
A broken provenance chain does not automatically invalidate publication but requires editorial assessment.

## IF-17 — Network interpretation is contextual
Flow patterns must be assessed by topic, fact class, jurisdiction and time period.

## IF-18 — No automatic disinformation label
Flow anomalies can trigger investigation but cannot by themselves establish manipulation or disinformation.

## IF-19 — Preserve historical states
Material flow states and transformations must remain auditable after corrections, updates and revisions.

## IF-20 — Human editorial gate
Information-flow intelligence informs investigation and verification; it never independently authorizes publication, correction, retraction or blame attribution.
