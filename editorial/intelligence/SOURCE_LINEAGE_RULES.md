# SOURCE NETWORK & LINEAGE RULES

## LN-01 — Lineage before source count
Visible publications are not independent evidence unless their underlying evidentiary ancestry is independent.

## LN-02 — Independence is proposition-specific
Never label two sources globally independent. Record independence for the precise claim/evidence relationship assessed.

## LN-03 — Common ancestry collapses corroboration
Shared dispatch, document, dataset, witness, media, parent feed or underlying evidence must be treated as one lineage for independence analysis.

## LN-04 — Preserve every node
Collapsing lineages for analysis must never delete or merge the underlying source, publication, evidence or claim records.

## LN-05 — Primary evidence prevails
Where available, trace the network to the original document, dataset, observation, ruling, statement, image, video or other primary evidence.

## LN-06 — Aggregation is not independence
An aggregator, syndicator or secondary article can improve discovery or context without creating an independent evidentiary lineage.

## LN-07 — Repetition is not corroboration
Repeated wording, quotations or claims do not constitute independent corroboration when the underlying evidence is shared.

## LN-08 — Contradiction must remain visible
Independent contradictory lineages must not be averaged, silently resolved or discarded.

## LN-09 — Chronology matters
A later publication cannot be treated as independent confirmation of information that originated from the same later source.

## LN-10 — Network density is not truth
A dense graph does not establish truth. A sparse graph does not establish falsity.

## LN-11 — Centrality is not quality
A highly central source is not automatically more authoritative, accurate or suitable for every fact class.

## LN-12 — Common lineage is not proof of coordination
Shared ancestry may result from ordinary syndication or common evidence. Malicious coordination must never be inferred from network structure alone.

## LN-13 — Material concentration triggers review
If a consequential claim depends materially on one lineage, flag the case for editorial assessment even when many visible publications exist.

## LN-14 — Network anomalies require human assessment
Unexplained lineage changes, reused evidence, temporal anomalies and contradictory branches are alerts, not automatic conclusions.

## LN-15 — Historical network state is preserved
Lineage relationships, assessments and alerts are time-indexed and must remain auditable after later changes.

## LN-16 — No automatic publication decision
Network intelligence informs detection and verification; it cannot automatically authorize publication, correction, retraction or closure.

## LN-17 — Fact-class fitness remains separate
A source may be highly useful for discovery but unsuitable as primary evidence for a specific fact class.

## LN-18 — Source health propagates through lineage
Material source correction, retraction, provenance or integrity events must trigger assessment of dependent lineages and cases.

## LN-19 — Confidence and independence are distinct
High confidence from one strong primary lineage is not equivalent to high confidence from multiple independent primary lineages; record both dimensions.

## LN-20 — Optimize for evidentiary diversity
The strategic objective is not maximum source volume but sufficient independent, traceable and fit-for-purpose evidentiary lineages.
