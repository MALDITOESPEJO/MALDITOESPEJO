# MALDITOESPEJO — VERIFICATION RECORD SCHEMA

## Purpose

Define the minimum contents of an editorial verification record associated with an article marked `verified` or `published`.

The record is an audit document. It does not replace human editorial judgement.

## Required sections

Every verification record must identify:

1. `article_id`
2. publication date
3. section and author
4. editorial status
5. publication sources actually used
6. claim-to-evidence assessment
7. temporal check
8. corroboration / contradiction check
9. interpretation risks
10. unresolved points
11. human approval status

## Source rule

A discovery source is not automatically a publication source. The record must identify the evidence actually examined and used to support the published assertions.

## Claim rule

Important public assertions must be traceable to evidence. The record should distinguish:

- established facts;
- statements attributed to a person or institution;
- context;
- unknown or pending matters.

## Status rule

`verified` means the documented verification work has been completed to the level required by the editorial process. It does not mean that human publication approval has been granted.

`published` requires, in addition, the separate Publication Gate and explicit human editorial approval.

## Plain-language rule

The public article should explain conclusions in ordinary language. Internal labels, scoring systems and technical verification terminology should remain internal unless publication requires otherwise.

> **La investigación puede ser compleja; la explicación no debe serlo.**
